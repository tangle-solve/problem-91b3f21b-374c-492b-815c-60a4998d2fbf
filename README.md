### CHEM-000492: Testing hyperconjugation as the cause of solvent-enhanced carbon tunneling
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Test whether coordination-dependent geminal hyperconjugation explains tunneling-rate changes in the benzene oxide/oxepin system. Independently constrain coordination populations and distinguish barrier narrowing from energetic stabilization.
- **Status:** The 2025 calculations connect coordination motifs to C–C shortening and enhanced tunneling. Solvent-controlled tunneling already has experimental precedents; the open target is this proposed electronic cause and its quantitative prediction.
